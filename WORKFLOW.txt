4. RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARIES
----------------------------

  SQLite
    Owns authoritative documents, entities, and structured metadata.

  ChromaDB
    Owns embedded chunks and semantic nearest-neighbor retrieval.

  Semantic retrieval
    Converts a query into list[EvidenceChunk] with raw Chroma distances.

  Retrieval sufficiency
    Filters existing EvidenceChunk objects using explicit thresholds and
    reports whether enough usable evidence exists. It does not alter raw
    distances, generate confidence scores, or judge analytic correctness.

  Reasoning/fallback
    Future pipeline stages; intentionally absent from the current repository.
