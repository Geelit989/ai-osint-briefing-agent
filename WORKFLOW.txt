4. RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARIES
----------------------------

  SQLite
    Owns authoritative documents, entities, and structured metadata.

  ChromaDB
    Owns embedded chunks and semantic nearest-neighbor retrieval.

  Semantic retrieval
    Converts a query into list[EvidenceChunk] with raw Chroma distances.

  Retrieval sufficiency
    Filters existing EvidenceChunk objects using explicit thresholds, resolves
    authoritative documents from SQLite, groups only deterministically proven
    record identities, and reports whether enough independent evidence units
    exist. It does not alter raw distances, generate confidence scores, or
    judge analytic correctness.

  Independent Evidence Unit
    A deterministic sufficiency-accounting construct, not proof of independent
    sourcing or corroboration. Provider diversity is not an identity signal;
    same-provider reporting is not automatically duplicate reporting. Similar
    or related reporting remains separate unless exact identity is proven.

  Reasoning/fallback
    Reasoning receives every usable chunk, including duplicate chunks grouped
    into one accounting unit. Claim-support validation runs after synthesis.
